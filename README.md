# Excel IF, AND, OR & NOT Functions

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains my Excel lab assignment based on **Logical Functions**.

The assignment focuses on using:

- IF Function
- AND Function
- OR Function
- NOT Function
- Combination of multiple logical functions

These functions are used to analyze student records based on conditions such as GPA, age, scholarship status, Math and English results, and attendance rate.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains student information such as:

- Student Name
- Age
- GPA
- Math Result
- English Result
- Scholarship Status
- Attendance Rate

## 🧮 Functions Used

### IF
Used to return different results based on whether a condition is TRUE or FALSE.

### AND
Used when **all conditions must be TRUE**.

### OR
Used when **at least one condition must be TRUE**.

### NOT
Used to reverse a logical condition.

## 📝 Assignment Conditions

The assignment includes conditions such as:

1. Students who did NOT pass English OR have a GPA below 3.0.
2. Students who passed both Math and English.
3. Students who have a scholarship AND attendance of 85% or higher.
4. Students who either passed Math OR have a GPA of 3.5 or higher, but NOT both.
5. Students who did NOT pass Math AND did NOT pass English.
6. Students aged 20 or younger AND having a GPA of 3.5 or higher.
7. Students who have a scholarship OR passed both Math and English, with attendance of at least 80%.
8. Students who did NOT get a scholarship AND have a GPA below 3.0, OR have attendance below 70%.

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this assignment is to understand how Excel logical functions can be combined to evaluate multiple conditions and analyze student data efficiently.
